DATA

Reads Polymarket's public feed

Order book, executed-trade history, and metadata across 20,000+ markets. Non-custodial, unaffiliated, read-only — it never touches your funds.

BUILD

Reconstructs candles from raw trades

OHLC plus tick candles — one candle per N executed trades instead of per fixed time. Genuinely the right primitive for bursty market activity.

READ

Trader-grade analytics on top

Technical indicators, orderflow / footprint (buy vs. sell volume per price level), and volume profile — tools Polymarket's own UI doesn't give you.

SCAN

Backtest, then scan every market

Write a strategy in TypeScript against history; the scanner runs it across all 20,000+ markets in <3 min, ranked by net P&L. This is the real moat.

$

Freemium, no execution

Free charts + indicators; Pro at $19.99/mo adds custom indicators and backtesting. You still place trades on Polymarket itself.

02

Keep the sharp edges. Break off the dull ones.

Six things it gets right, next to the nine that hold it back.

Keep 6 strengths

01Legible positioning

"TradingView for prediction markets" needs no explanation to the target user.

02Tick candles

The correct primitive for bursty, uneven prediction-market flow. Smart and defensible.

03Orderflow & volume profile

Real pro tooling absent from the native Polymarket interface.

04Market-wide P&L scanner

Turns a charting toy into an edge-finding engine. The single best idea here.

05Freemium, no card

A genuinely useful free tier is a low-friction hook.

06Non-custodial & read-only

No fund custody → minimal legal and regulatory surface. Do not give this up.

Fix / Kill 9 weaknesses

01Brand collision

"Truth Terminal" is already a famous crypto-AI bot; "TruthTickTerminal" is three loaded words and hard to type.Fix → rebrand to a short, platform-neutral name.

02Polymarket-only

Forfeits the richest edge of all: cross-venue pricing vs. Kalshi.Fix → go multi-venue early.

03No execution

"Go trade elsewhere" betrays the word terminal.Fix → non-custodial one-click execution (bring your own key).

04Backtesting needs code

TypeScript locks the killer feature to programmers.Fix → no-code + AI strategy builder; TS as the escape hatch.

05The scanner overfits

Ranking 20,000 strategies by P&L surfaces noise; reconstructed OHLC ignores slippage & fees.Fix → model fills/fees, walk-forward validation, overfit warnings.

06"Zero delay" is unprovable

Reconstructed feeds can lag.Fix → show data freshness & latency openly.

07No alerts, no mobile

Sharps live on alerts and on their phones.Fix → alerting engine + mobile/PWA.

08No smart-money tracking

Whale & copy features are among the most-used in the ecosystem.Fix → whale tracker + copy-signal feed.

09Thin price ladder

Flat $19.99 with fuzzy gating.Fix → clearer tiers + usage metering on scans.

03

The version worth building

Same strong core — now it closes the loop from signal to fill, across venues.

Positioning: the professional analytics and execution terminal for prediction markets — across venues. Keep tick candles, orderflow, volume profile, the market-wide scanner, and the non-custodial stance. Then add the pieces that make it a terminal instead of a viewer.

MULTI-VENUE

Polymarket + Kalshi, unified

One market view, a cross-venue arbitrage finder, and consensus odds — the edge no single-venue tool can offer.

EXECUTION

Non-custodial one-click fills

Bring your own API key or wallet. Trade from the chart without ever handing over custody of funds.

STRATEGY

No-code + AI builder

Describe a strategy in plain language or click it together. Rigorous validation — slippage, fees, walk-forward, a confidence score — so backtests tell the truth.

SIGNALS

Alerts, whales & copy

Push alerts on mispricing, odds swings, whale prints and resolution deadlines. Track smart-money wallets and mirror their moves.

TRUST

Honesty as a feature

Visible data-freshness indicators, modeled fills instead of fantasy fills, and explicit overfit warnings. In a space full of unprovable "zero-delay" claims, credibility is the differentiator.

The one-line thesis: TruthTick built a great telescope and asked you to fly to the planet yourself. Sharpline is the telescope and the launch button — pointed at every venue at once.

04

How it ships

Four phases, each a standalone reason to sign up.

PHASE 1MVP · Free

Polymarket OHLC + tick charts, orderflow, volume profile, live trades, watchlists, and basic alerts. The free hook, better than the original's.

PHASE 2Pro

No-code strategy builder, validated backtesting, the market-wide scanner, and non-custodial one-click execution on Polymarket.

PHASE 3Multi-venue

Kalshi integration → cross-venue arbitrage and consensus odds. Whale tracking, copy signals, and a mobile/PWA app.

PHASE 4Automation

Strategy automation, a public API, and team workspaces for funds and groups.

05

A name that can carry it

"TruthTick Terminal" collides with a famous crypto bot, runs three words long, and drags in loaded connotations. It has to go.

Sharplinerecommended

A sharp is the trade term for a professional, informed bettor — exactly the user, exactly the "trade like a pro" pitch, minus the baggage. A line is both the betting line and the chart line. One word, ownable, easy to type, and platform-agnostic across Polymarket and Kalshi.

Oddscope

Odds + scope. Analytical and scannable; reads as a research instrument.

Edgewire

"Edge," the trader's advantage, over a live "wire" feed.

Vane

A weathervane reads which way the market is turning. Short, modern, brandable.

Note on Polyview: fine as a working title, but "Poly" ties it to Polymarket — which quietly contradicts the multi-venue strategy. Pick a name that can hold Kalshi too.
